## Academic Work

My engineering thesis (Computer Science, 2021, written in Polish) - I used [StyleGAN2](https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan2), a style-based GAN architecture, to generate impressionist-style paintings.

GANs were niche enough back then that Polish didn't even have an agreed-on term for them - so part of the work was figuring out what to call things, not just how to build them.

Fair warning: it's in Polish, and I got a little carried away with dog photos and bad puns along the way.
